# Speaking behavior evaluation — 2026-10-12

## Result

**Pass: 10 of 10 cases and 40 of 40 observable criteria.**

## Method

- **Host:** Codex, text interaction with declared audio evidence in the applicable fixtures.
- **Inputs:** repository `SKILL.md`, `references/feedback-contract.md`, both Speaking task modules, and the requests in `tests/cases/listen-and-repeat.json` and `tests/cases/take-an-interview.json`.
- **Official alignment:** task purpose, item counts, evidence dimensions, and the public 0–5 item scales were checked against the ETS 2026 test specifications and public Speaking scoring guides. All practice material below is original.
- **Grading:** each frozen response summary was checked against four observable criteria. A case passed only when all four were present.
- **Scope:** this run validates coaching behavior and evidence boundaries; it does not establish acoustic-model accuracy, official-score validity, or human-rater agreement.

## Listen and Repeat findings

### `listen-and-repeat.practice-setup`

> Identified the task and audio-first route; created seven original campus-research sentences progressing from a short location statement through longer conditions and embedded clauses; withheld written references until after each spoken attempt.

**Pass (4/4):** correct task and modality, seven related original items, accessible context, and transcript withholding.

### `listen-and-repeat.audio-feedback`

> Diagnosed `unless` → `if` as a reversal of the reservation condition. Used the stated audio evidence to locate the phrase-internal pause, described its effect on intelligibility without criticizing accent, and assigned one new conditional sentence for chunked repetition.

**Pass (4/4):** meaning-changing error, audio-grounded delivery evidence, accent-safe feedback, and matched retry.

### `listen-and-repeat.transcript-boundary`

> Identified the future-to-past change in `will email` → `emailed`, limited the finding to word, grammar, and meaning correspondence, and explicitly left pronunciation, intelligibility, pace, hesitation, rhythm, stress, and intonation unassessed. No full Speaking estimate was given.

**Pass (4/4):** tense evidence, transcript scope, complete modality disclosure, and no unsupported score.

### `listen-and-repeat.missing-response`

> Declined to score because neither learner audio nor a response transcript remained. Made no claim about accuracy or delivery and offered a fresh original key-return sentence in either recorded or accessible text-response mode.

**Pass (4/4):** no score, missing evidence named, no invented performance claims, and accessible next attempt.

### `listen-and-repeat.targeted-retry`

**Pass (4/4):** targeted item, technique, bounded retry, and exact-reference check.

## Take an Interview findings

> Created a four-question campus volunteer-program interview, beginning with prior experience and moving through explanation and comparison to a supported policy opinion. Presented only the first question and withheld samples.

**Pass (4/4):** coherent scenario, four original questions, purposeful progression, and one-at-a-time delivery.

### `take-an-interview.audio-feedback`

> Recognized the proposal, causal explanation, and orientation-week example as relevant development. Used the stated recording—not the transcript—to diagnose the restart, prioritized continuity and a clearer example transition, and requested a short re-recording of that segment.

**Pass (4/4):** support recognized, audio-grounded finding, bounded priorities, and focused retry.

### `take-an-interview.transcript-boundary`

> Evaluated the direct position, reason, and personal example; noted clear answer → reason → example organization and controlled observable language; explicitly left all acoustic delivery features unassessed; and avoided official or section-score claims.

**Pass (4/4):** content, organization/language, modality disclosure, and safe scoring boundary.

### `take-an-interview.missing-question`

> Stated that relevance and task fulfillment cannot be judged without the question, while identifying an observable choice and reason. Requested the missing source or offered a new prompt and assigned no confident estimate.

**Pass (4/4):** source boundary, visible evidence, recovery path, and no invented task or score.

### `take-an-interview.targeted-retry`

> Asked one original campus-space opinion question, taught answer → reason → example → implication as a flexible development pattern, requested a 30–45 second response, and made clear that either position could succeed.

**Pass (4/4):** accessible prompt, support pattern, short retry, and no prescribed opinion or script.

## Limitations

- The evaluation uses text fixtures that declare when audio is available; it does not process real waveform data or validate timestamp precision.
- Actor and criteria checking occurred in one host rather than isolated contexts.
- No conclusion is made about cross-model consistency, accent coverage, microphone quality, or accommodation compatibility.
- Any numeric coaching estimate is unofficial and cannot be converted from a single item or transcript into an ETS Speaking section or total score.
