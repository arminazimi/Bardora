# Feedback contract

Use this contract whenever evaluating a learner response. Adapt the wording to the learner, but preserve the information.

## Required output

1. **Task and scope** — task type, response modality, and what evidence was available.
2. **What worked** — one or two strengths tied to quoted or precisely located evidence.
3. **Top priorities** — no more than three changes, ordered by likely impact.
4. **How to improve** — a concrete correction, technique, or original example for each priority.
5. **Practice estimate, if useful** — named scale, confidence or uncertainty, and the statement that it is not an official ETS score.
6. **Retry** — one bounded revision request or short follow-up task.

## Evidence rules

- Distinguish an observed feature from an inference.
- Tie every criticism to a response excerpt, timestamp, or clearly described omission.
- When only a transcript is available, limit Speaking feedback to content and observable language.
- When a task depends on source material, judge accuracy only against source material actually provided.

## Calibration

Prefer qualitative bands until a task-specific rubric has been implemented and validated. A numeric estimate must name its scale and explain the material limitations that affect confidence. Never convert a partial-task result into a total TOEFL score.
