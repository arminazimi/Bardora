# Bardora brand system

## Brand foundation

**Name:** Bardora  
**Tagline:** The modern art of English mastery.  
**Logo concept:** Scholar Portal

Bardora is the independent public identity for this open-source English-learning project. The brand name must be used as the product name. TOEFL® may be used only to describe test compatibility and learning context, together with the repository's non-affiliation language.

The Scholar Portal combines an open circular threshold with a geometric `B`. The outer portal represents entry into a broader learning ecosystem; the two bowls represent structured progression; the openings keep the system approachable and ongoing rather than closed or final.

Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least one outer-stroke width. Do not rotate it, close its portal openings, add effects, recolor individual pieces outside the approved palette, or use the primary mark below 32 px when the compact icon is available.

## Academic Slate palette

The palette moves through one blue-slate family from ink to ivory. Neutral colors should carry most of the interface; Scholar Blue is an emphasis color, not a large decorative fill.

| Token | Hex | Role | Suggested share |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Bardora Ink | `#111820` | Primary text, dark surfaces | 30% |
| Deep Slate | `#263640` | Supporting surfaces and secondary text | 22% |
| Scholar Blue | `#466272` | Active states, progress, emphasis | 12% |
| Quiet Mist | `#A9B5B9` | Borders and inactive states | 11% |
| Paper Ivory | `#F4F1EA` | Reading surfaces and light backgrounds | 25% |

Color must never be the only way instructional meaning, progress, correctness, or errors are communicated.

## Voice

Bardora should sound calm, precise, encouraging, and evidence-based. Prefer a clear next action over motivational filler. The tagline is reserved for brand-level surfaces; coaching responses should use the learner's immediate goal instead.
