# Classical brand-name scan — 2026-10-13

## Purpose and limits

This note explores a second naming direction for the project: a short, globally pronounceable master brand with a classical or ancient-wisdom story, followed by a plain descriptor such as **“AI preparation for the TOEFL® test.”** The approved visual direction and line remain unchanged:

> **Ancient insight. Modern mastery.**

The selected palette remains deep lapis, turquoise, warm ivory, saffron gold, and restrained burgundy.

This is a product-naming scan, not legal advice or a trademark-clearance opinion. Search results, app-store indexing, domain RDAP responses, and package-registry responses are snapshots. They can miss unindexed, unregistered, pending, local, or confusingly similar rights.

## Executive recommendation

The most interesting genuinely new candidate from this round is **Vohumera** (`voh-hoo-ME-ra`). It is coined from **Vohu Manah**, the Avestan concept commonly rendered “Good Thought” or “Good Mind,” with a smoother international ending. Encyclopaedia Iranica describes *Vohu Manah* as the good moral state of mind and identifies it as an ancient personified concept. That makes the story unusually relevant to disciplined learning without naming the product after a crowded famous god. [Encyclopaedia Iranica: Vohu Manah](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/bahman-vohu-manah/bahman-i-in-the-avesta/)

Recommended presentation:

> **VOHUMERA**  
> AI preparation for the TOEFL® test  
> *Ancient insight. Modern mastery.*

Why it advances:

- It has an authentic ancient-Iranian idea behind it, not a randomly “mythic-sounding” construction.
- “Good mind” connects naturally to learning, judgment, feedback, and mastery across all four language skills.
- It does not sound writing-only or vocabulary-only.
- Exact web and indexed app-store searches found no obvious education, language, or AI product using the name.
- On 2026-10-13, the authoritative `.com` and `.ai` RDAP services returned `404` for `vohumera.com` and `vohumera.ai`; GitHub user, npm package, and PyPI project checks also returned `404`. These are unreserved-looking snapshots, not guarantees.

**Chistava** remains the strongest emotionally accessible Iranian option and may still win customer testing. **Aretaya** is the strongest Greek-derived alternative. **Lexithea** is the most immediately appealing continuation of the founder’s “Lexa/Lexia” preference, but its similarity to the established education brand **Lexia** creates a meaningful clearance concern; it should not be the default without professional review.

## Root material used responsibly

The strongest names in this list begin with roots whose meanings are supported by established reference works:

| Root | Supported meaning | Naming use |
|---|---|---|
| **Vohu Manah** | “Good Thought” / the good moral state of mind | **Vohumera**, **Vohumana** |
| **Čistā / Čisti** | Connected to “notice, understand”; *čisti* carries senses such as intuition or idea | **Chistava** |
| **Daēnā** | Conscience, vision, religion, and in later exposition wisdom/teaching | **Daenavia**, **Daenora** |
| **aretē** | Goodness or excellence of any kind | **Aretaya**, **Aretevia** |
| **logos** | Word, speech, discourse, reasoning | **Logion**, **Logeira** |
| **sōphrosynē** | Soundness of mind, moderation, discretion | **Sophraya** |
| **lexis / thea** | Expression or diction; goddess | **Lexithea** |
| **paideia** | Training, teaching, education, culture | **Paideora** |

Sources: [Iranica on Čistā](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/cista-and-cisti-avestan-derivatives-of-the-verb-cit-to-notice-to-understand/), [Iranica on Dēn/Daēnā](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/den/), [Perseus LSJ on aretē](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Aabo%3Asec%2C00010%3Aa%29reth%2F), [Perseus LSJ on logos](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.04.0057%3Aentry%3Dlo%2Fgos), [Perseus on sōphrosynē](https://atlas.perseus.tufts.edu/dictionaries/headword/%CF%83%CF%89%CF%86%CF%81%CE%BF%CF%83%CF%8D%CE%BD%CE%B7/), [Perseus on thea](https://atlas.perseus.tufts.edu/dictionaries/entry/urn%3Acite2%3Ascaife-viewer%3Adictionaries.v1%3Alsj-n47950/), and [Perseus on paideia](https://atlas.perseus.tufts.edu/dictionaries/entry/urn%3Acite2%3Ascaife-viewer%3Adictionary-entries.atlas_v1%3Amiddle-liddell.perseus-eng2-n24088/).

These coined names are **inspired by** the roots; they should not be marketed as exact ancient words when they are not.

### Recommendation in plain language

- Choose **Vohumera** if the priority is a globally ownable, premium ecosystem with a genuine ancient-Iranian idea.
- Choose **Chistava** if warmth, ease of storytelling, and Iranian identity matter more than maximum abstraction.
- Choose **Aretaya** if the desired personality is Greek, aspirational, lighter, and less explicitly spiritual.
- Keep **Lexithea** only as a taste benchmark until a professional confusing-similarity search addresses Lexia.

## Exact-search and marketplace observations

Searches used quoted candidate names combined with education, language, AI, app, App Store, and Google Play terms. For **Chistava, Vohumera, Aretaya,** and **Sophraya**, no obvious indexed education/language/AI product or app-store listing was found in this pass. That absence is not exhaustive and does not establish rights.

The exact **Lexithea** result set was dominated by a racehorse of that name rather than software or education. That is a relatively minor category collision, but the more important issue is the near-name **Lexia**, whose official company page describes a long-running literacy and language-learning business. [Lexia official company page](https://www.lexialearning.com/company/)

Several initially attractive constructions failed quickly because exact search revealed live products in the same or adjacent category: Paideora in children’s educational stories, Nousara in tutoring analytics, Phronara in enterprise AI, and Veritara in AI. This is why a mythic sound alone is not enough.

## How the TOEFL purpose stays obvious

The classical word should be the **master brand**, not the whole explanation. The category should be communicated with a consistent descriptor:

> **VOHUMERA**  
> AI preparation for the TOEFL® test

The first screen or repository description should immediately say:

> Practice Reading, Listening, Speaking, and Writing with an independent AI coach built for the TOEFL® test.

This lets the product own a distinctive name while remaining understandable in under three seconds. It also leaves room for later modules such as **Vohumera Coach**, **Vohumera Pro**, **Vohumera Classroom**, and **Vohumera Institute**.

ETS states that its marks must not be incorporated into company names, product names, domain names, social-media identities, or similar identifiers, and provides rules for informational use and non-endorsement language. The public lockup and landing page should be reviewed against the current guidance before launch. [ETS trademark guidelines](https://www.ets.org/legal/trademarks.html)

## Recommended customer test

Do not decide from founder preference or desk research alone. Test these three:

1. **Vohumera** — deep ancient-Iranian concept and maximum distinctiveness.
2. **Chistava** — warm Iranian concept with voice and understanding.
3. **Aretaya** — lighter Greek idea of excellence.

Show each participant only the name first, then the descriptor. With 20–30 target learners from different first-language groups, measure:

- pronunciation on first sight;
- spelling after hearing the name once;
- unaided recall after ten minutes;
- perceived trust for a high-stakes exam;
- “What do you think this product does?” before and after the descriptor;
- preference after seeing the same theme and product promise.

Keep **Lexithea** as an optional fourth “taste” candidate, but do not let survey popularity override the Lexia clearance concern.

## Decision proposal

Advance **Vohumera**, **Chistava**, and **Aretaya** to a small learner test. Preserve the approved identity system for all three:

- theme: **Ancient Wisdom, Modern Mastery**;
- line: **Ancient insight. Modern mastery.**;
- colors: lapis, turquoise, ivory, saffron gold, restrained burgundy;
- tone: scholarly, encouraging, global, and modern—not a museum replica or fantasy game.

If a decision had to be made from desk research alone, select **Vohumera** as the new lead and keep **Chistava** as the more immediately approachable alternative. Before any rename, domain purchase, public announcement, or paid launch, conduct professional exact and confusing-similarity trademark clearance in the intended markets and classes.
