# TOEFL iBT® test 2026 task-type matrix

**Verified:** October 12, 2026  
**Format effective:** January 21, 2026

This matrix summarizes current public ETS information. The current test-taker page is the authority for learner-facing totals; the technical blueprint supplies task-level detail. ETS notes that time and item counts may vary as the test adapts.

## Current section structure

| Section | Task types | Published items (may vary) | Approx. base time | Delivery |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Reading | 3 | 50 | 30 minutes | Two-stage adaptive |
| Listening | 4 | 47 | 29 minutes | Two-stage adaptive |
| Writing | 3 | 12 | 23 minutes | Linear |
| Speaking | 2 | 11 | 8 minutes | Linear |

Directions are excluded from these times. ETS advises allowing approximately two hours for the overall test appointment and notes that Reading and Listening may vary with adaptation.

## Score reporting

- Four section scores and an overall score use a **1–6 scale in 0.5 increments**, aligned to CEFR.
- The overall score is the average of the four section scores, rounded to the nearest half band under ETS rules.
- During the two-year transition through January 2028, reports also show a comparable overall score on the 0–120 scale.
- ETS sets no universal passing score; institutions set their own requirements.
- Scores are valid for two years.

The coach must not reverse-engineer an official conversion or present a practice estimate as an ETS score.
